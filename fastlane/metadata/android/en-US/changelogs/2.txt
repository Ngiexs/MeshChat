Initial beta release.

- End-to-end encrypted group chat over Tor
- No account, no phone number, no email
- Automatic host failover via backup nodes
- Disappearing messages (30s, 5m, 1h, 1d)
- Reply to specific messages
- Delivery receipts
- QR code sharing for joining a group
- Multiple groups per device
